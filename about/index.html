The founder

Robert Ceasar

Founder & CEO, BannedProduct Media Inc.

Robert Ceasar is a U.S. Army veteran, BI developer, AWS Architect, creator, father and entrepreneur. He served as an 11B Infantryman and later reclassified to 68S Preventive Medicine Specialist after being injured during a deployment to Afghanistan.

From 2018 to 2022, Robert worked as a contracted Live Stream influencer with KingsGroup (FunPlus) for two of their major titles, State of Survival and King of Avalon — giving him a firsthand understanding of the creator economy.

In 2022 he started IT school, and later went on to serve as a Business Intelligence Developer on the Wells Fargo Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB) Data Integrity team, working at one of the largest financial institutions in the world.

He founded BannedProduct Media Inc. to help creators, small businesses, and bold brands build platforms that refuse to stay quiet. His work bridges the gap between storytelling and systems — helping clients create stronger content, build better websites, automate repetitive work, and turn ideas into platforms.

Why "BannedProduct"

It represents creators, people, veterans, entrepreneurs, survivors, and truth-tellers who have been dismissed, misunderstood, censored, underestimated, or treated like a problem for speaking honestly.

BannedProduct means being labeled, rejected, counted out or "Banned" — simply by being thought of as a statistic and a "product" of your environment — and turning that into fuel.

BannedProduct Media Inc. was built on the belief that real stories deserve a platform — especially the ones people are told not to tell.

We create. We educate. We advocate. We build.
