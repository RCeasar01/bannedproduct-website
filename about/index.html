Our Mission

Amplify the Unapologetic

BannedProduct Media Inc. exists to create a space where real stories are told, real businesses are built, and real voices carry weight — regardless of platform politics.

We were forged in service and tempered by entrepreneurship. Every project we touch carries the same values: discipline, precision, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

From podcasts that move people to dashboards that drive decisions — we do the work that matters.

The Brand

BannedProduct Is More Than a Name.
It Is a Statement.

It represents creators, people, veterans, entrepreneurs, survivors, and truth-tellers who have been dismissed, misunderstood, censored, underestimated, or treated like a problem for speaking honestly.

BannedProduct Media Inc. was built on the belief that real stories deserve a platform — especially the ones people are told not to tell. We create bold media, powerful digital platforms, and intelligent systems for individuals, creators, and brands that refuse to stay quiet.

BannedProduct means being labeled, rejected, or counted out — and turning that into fuel.

We are not here to blend in.
We are here to create bold, tell real stories, and build platforms that cannot be ignored.

Robert Ceasar

Founder & CEO, BannedProduct Media Inc.

Robert Ceasar is a U.S. Army veteran, BI developer, AWS Architect, creator, father, entrepreneur, and parents' rights advocate. He served as an 11B Infantryman and later reclassified to 68S Preventive Medicine Specialist after being injured during a deployment to Afghanistan. He founded BannedProduct Media Inc. to help creators, small businesses, and bold brands build platforms that refuse to stay quiet.

Before building his tech and media career, Robert competed as a professional contracted video game player and established himself as an influencer and broadcaster — giving him a firsthand understanding of the creator economy long before it had a name. That foundation, combined with military leadership, business intelligence, cloud technology, and media production, is what makes his approach to every project different. His work bridges the gap between storytelling and systems — helping clients create stronger content, build better websites, automate repetitive work, and turn ideas into platforms.

BannedProduct Media was built for creators and businesses that want more than generic content or basic tech support. It is a media and technology company focused on bold storytelling, practical strategy, and tools that help people grow, connect, and stand out.
