Effective Date: June 9, 2026 · Applies to all BannedProduct Media giveaways

These Official Rules ("Rules") govern all giveaways, sweepstakes, contests, and promotional events ("Giveaway") conducted by BannedProduct Media Inc. ("Sponsor," "we," "our," "us"). By entering any Giveaway, you agree to be bound by these Rules and by all decisions of the Sponsor, which are final and binding.

1. Sponsor

BannedProduct Media Inc.
Website: bannedproductmedia.com
Email: giveaways@bannedproductmedia.com

2. Eligibility

Giveaways are open to legal residents of the fifty (50) United States and the District of Columbia who are at least eighteen (18) years of age at the time of entry, unless a specific Giveaway states different eligibility requirements.

The following individuals are NOT eligible to participate:

  • Employees, officers, and directors of BannedProduct Media Inc.
  • Immediate family members (spouse, parent, child, sibling) of the above
  • Anyone living in the same household as the above, whether related or not
  • Persons who have won a BannedProduct Media giveaway within the past 30 days (unless otherwise specified)

Void where prohibited by law. It is the entrant's responsibility to determine whether they are eligible to participate under the laws of their jurisdiction.

3. Entry Period

Each Giveaway has its own specified entry period, announced on the relevant platform (Whatnot, social media, or website). Entries submitted outside the specified period will not be considered. The Sponsor's clock is the official timekeeping device for each Giveaway.

4. How to Enter

Entry methods vary by Giveaway and will be clearly stated at the time of each promotion. Common entry methods include:

  • Whatnot Chat Entry: Typing a specified keyword (e.g., "!GIVEAWAY") in the live stream chat during a designated entry window while following the Sponsor's Whatnot account
  • Social Media Entry: Following specified accounts, liking, sharing, or commenting as described in the individual Giveaway post
  • Website Entry: Completing an entry form at bannedproductmedia.com/giveaways

Limit of one (1) entry per person per Giveaway unless otherwise stated. Use of multiple accounts to enter is prohibited and will result in disqualification. No purchase is necessary to enter or win.

5. Prize

5.1 Prize Description

Each Giveaway's prize(s) will be clearly described on the applicable Giveaway announcement. Prizes may include but are not limited to electronics, gift cards, merchandise, services, or digital goods.

5.2 Prize Value

The approximate retail value (ARV) of each prize will be stated in the Giveaway announcement. Actual value may vary. No cash equivalent or substitution of prizes is permitted except at the Sponsor's sole discretion.

5.3 Prize Restrictions

Prizes are non-transferable and cannot be sold, auctioned, traded, or bartered. The Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if the advertised prize becomes unavailable.

6. Winner Selection and Notification

6.1 Winner Selection

Winners are selected by random drawing from all eligible entries received during the entry period, unless the Giveaway specifies a skill-based selection method. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

6.2 Winner Notification

Potential winners will be notified:

  • Announced live on the applicable platform (Whatnot stream, etc.)
  • Via direct message on the platform where they entered
  • Via email if provided

Potential winners have forty-eight (48) hours from notification to respond and claim their prize. Failure to respond within 48 hours may result in forfeiture and selection of an alternate winner.

6.3 Verification

The potential winner may be required to verify their eligibility and provide shipping information and identification. The Sponsor reserves the right to verify eligibility before awarding any prize.

7. Claiming Your Prize

To receive a prize, the confirmed winner must:

  • Respond to the winner notification within 48 hours
  • Provide a valid US shipping address (for physical prizes)
  • Complete any required winner verification forms
  • For prizes with an ARV of $600 or more: provide a valid Social Security Number or Tax ID for IRS Form 1099 purposes

Physical prizes will be shipped within 14 business days of winner verification. The Sponsor is not responsible for lost, stolen, or undelivered prizes due to incorrect address information provided by the winner.

8. Taxes

All federal, state, and local taxes on prizes are the sole responsibility of the winner. The Sponsor will report prize winnings to the IRS as required by law. Winners should consult a tax advisor regarding the tax implications of prize receipt.

The following conduct is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate disqualification:

  • Using multiple accounts, bots, scripts, or automated methods to enter
  • Providing false information during entry or prize claim
  • Harassing, threatening, or disparaging the Sponsor, its employees, or other participants
  • Entering on behalf of another person without their knowledge and consent
  • Any attempt to manipulate the selection process
