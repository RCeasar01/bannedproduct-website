Common Questions

FAQ

How quickly do you respond to inquiries? +

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days. For urgent projects, note that in your message and we'll prioritize accordingly.

Do you work with clients outside the US? +

Yes — we work with clients worldwide on all remote services (podcast production, web development, BI, AI automation). On-location services like photography and videography are primarily US-based.

Do you offer payment plans? +

We offer flexible payment arrangements for larger projects. Typically 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Monthly retainer arrangements are also available for ongoing services.

How do I apply to be a podcast guest? +

Use the contact form above and select "Podcast Guest Application" from the service dropdown. Tell us your story and why you'd be a great fit for BannedProduct Talks. We review all applications personally.
